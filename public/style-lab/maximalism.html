01Where exactly is the festival?

Mahalaxmi Lawns, off Nagar Road, Pune. Shuttles run every 20 minutes from Kalyani Nagar and Koregaon Park between 1 p.m. and 5 a.m. The last shuttle leaves the venue 45 minutes after the final set.

02Can I leave and come back?

Yes — with a Full send or VIP wristband, re-entry is allowed all weekend until 2 a.m. Day passes are single-entry. If you need to step out for medicine or a phone charger, talk to the gate crew; they are nicer than they look.

03What should I bring?

Your ticket, a photo ID, a refillable bottle, sunscreen and shoes you can stand in for six hours. Leave the drone, the glass and the bad attitude at home. Lockers are available for ₹200 a day.

04Is the venue accessible?

All five stages have step-free routes and accessible viewing platforms. Accessible toilets are at every stage, and a companion ticket is free with any accessibility pass. Email us and we will walk you through the whole site before gates open.

05What if it rains?

We play through it. Pune in November is usually kind, but the main stage is covered and the Dome is indoors. In case of severe weather, sets may pause for safety — follow the crew and the screens, not the group chat.

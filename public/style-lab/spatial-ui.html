01 / On view

Five works.
No wrong side.

Windows, not frames. Open any work to step closer — each one holds its room, its material and the time it asks for.

5 works · All rooms · Orbit view

02 / The space

Built to be
wandered.

No wall labels, no fixed route, no headphones. The room does half the work — it is dim, quiet and deliberately a little too large.

01Light

2,700 K

Warm, low and indirect. The galleries dim to 1,800 K after 19:00 so the works can take over the room.

Dims after 19:00

02Sound

32 dBA

A room tone composed for the space — low enough to hear footsteps, never enough to mask the work.

No headphones

03Distance

4.5 m

Works are spaced so you never see two at once. The walk between them is part of the exhibition.

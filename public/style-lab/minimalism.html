02 / The studio

We design slowly,
so buildings
age quietly.

Twelve people in a converted warehouse in Pune. No pitch decks, no mood-board theatre. We start with a site model, a material sample and a budget we can actually hold onto.

Most of our work is for people who plan to stay. That changes the questions we ask — and the answers we are willing to build.

03 / How we work

Four seasons,
roughly.

Every project follows the same quiet rhythm. Timelines stretch; the order rarely does.

  1. 012–4 weeks

    Listen

    We walk the site, meet everyone who will use it, and write down what you actually need — not what a brochure says a house should have.

  2. 026–10 weeks

    Draw

    Plans, a physical model, one material board. We test the ugly options too, so the final drawing has nowhere left to hide.

  3. 038–14 months

    Make

    We stay on site weekly, check the junctions and protect the parts that matter when the budget starts negotiating.

  4. 041 week

    Hand over

    Keys, a folder of drawings and a manual for every finish. Then we come back after the first monsoon.

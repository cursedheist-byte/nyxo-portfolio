Ask us anything
What ages is Sprout for?

Most families start between four and nine. The path adapts its pace, so a five-year-old and a seven-year-old can use the same app at their own level.

How much screen time is it?

One lesson is about ten minutes. After it ends, the app says a calm goodbye and closes the activity — there is no autoplay and no “just one more”.

Is it safe for my child?

There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no chat features. Profiles are private, and the parent area sits behind a simple grown-up check.

What happens after the free trial?

You choose monthly at ₹349 or yearly at ₹2,999. If you do nothing, access simply pauses — we do not auto-charge an expired demo.
